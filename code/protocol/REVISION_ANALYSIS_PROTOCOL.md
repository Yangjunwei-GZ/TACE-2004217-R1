# Reviewer 1 revision analysis protocol

Frozen on 14 October 2026, after peer review and before inspection of revision model performance. This is a revision development protocol, not a claim of original prespecification.

## Purpose and evidence gate

Evaluate prognosis after initial TACE, and added predictive information beyond simple tumour size and intensity. No treatment-benefit claim is permitted. Independent radiologist phase/overlay review and a public code release remain separate human evidence requirements. Official file/phase provenance, automated physical geometry checks and generated review images must not be described as independent clinical review.

## Cohorts and images

Reconstruct all 233 public clinical records and all official 377 tumour masks. Preserve differences between metadata, actual archive entries and the old 195-patient/318-pair cache. Recover complete files from valid ZIPs or CRC-verified complete entries of the truncated local part-1 ZIP into the revision directory; never overwrite source archives. Use only same-patient, same-phase CT/mask pairs. Primary phase is arterial (code 1), selected for HCC enhancement and greatest source mask coverage (126 patients/230 masks in official metadata), before examining outcome performance. All expected arterial masks must be present, nonempty, within CT physical bounds, and geometrically coincident within 1e-4 numerical precision; substantive mismatches require review and are not automatically admitted. No cross-phase registration or header replacement. Restrict to official clinical IDs. Explicitly audit an extra archive mask if actual masks exceed official metadata. Supplementary mixed-phase recovery is descriptive and is not external validation.

Choose the largest arterial lesion by physical mask volume (voxel count times its original voxel volume, mm3). Total segmented tumour volume includes every available official arterial mask and is distinguished from total clinical tumour burden if not every clinical lesion is segmented. Compare voxel-ranked versus physical-volume-ranked selection.

## Extraction and covariates

Recover old extraction provenance; verify at least three examples against corrected output. Reuse verified corrected 107-feature rows with recorded provenance, and extract newly recovered eligible pairs with the same locked environment/settings. If settings reproduction fails, re-extract all eligible arterial pairs. Original image only; binWidth 25, no intensity normalisation, 1 mm isotropic resampling, B-spline CT interpolation, nearest-neighbour mask interpolation, positive labels binarised to 1 in memory. Save effective extractor settings, package versions, diagnostic metadata, ROI constraints and errors; no original mask is modified. Assess NIfTI scaling/anatomical distribution while distinguishing supportive HU evidence from inaccessible DICOM calibration.

Primary revised clinical baseline: age, female sex, Child-Pugh B versus A (source has no C), log1p(AFP), log1p(clinical lesion count), BCLC B versus A. Preserve original seven-variable baseline as a secondary reproducibility comparison, including redundancy diagnostics. Clinical portal-phase maximal axial diameter is separately compared with same-phase selected-lesion maximum 3D diameter. Tumour physical volume in cm3 is log1p transformed. Mean and median first-order CT intensities and 10th percentile are simple baseline measures. Derived treatment-response variables and total number of subsequent TACE sessions are excluded as post-baseline predictors. Albumin/bilirubin unit inconsistency is documented; baseline raw laboratory analyses are supplementary and do not silently assume g/L. Missing baseline values are median-imputed within training folds; zero-variance columns are removed within training folds. All corresponding comparisons share the same patients and resamples.

## Development and model comparison

Implement a transparent revised training-only development rule rather than claim exact reproduction of the historical five-candidate selection procedure. Train-only feature screening ranks all 107 features by univariable Harrell C-index departure from 0.5, filters constant features and Spearman correlation at absolute 0.85, retaining at most 12 ranked features. Inner three-fold patient-grouped cross-validation compares ridge Cox and two parsimonious Cox specifications: top-ranked two-feature Cox and elastic-net Cox on the two train-ranked features (l1_ratio 0.5). Ridge uses all surviving correlation-filtered features. Penalty grid 0.1, 1, 10 for ridge and 0.01, 0.1, 1 for two-feature specifications; choose highest inner validation concordance, ties within 0.01 favour fewer coefficients and stronger shrinkage. Recompute rankings, correlation filters, imputation and standardisation in each inner training fold. No HR>1 eligibility rule. All scores orient higher values to greater hazard as learned from training, without validation-directed sign reversal.

Baseline specifications: clinical; clinical+maximum 3D diameter; clinical+selected physical volume; clinical+total segmented physical volume; clinical+mean HU; clinical+10th percentile; clinical+diameter+mean HU; clinical+diameter+10th percentile; each size/intensity specification with a training-developed radiomics score; radiomics-only. Original clinical+score is secondary. Primary incremental comparison is clinical+diameter+mean HU with versus without the developed radiomics score. Clinical maximum axial diameter and median HU are additional prespecified sensitivity comparisons.

Fixed low Cox shrinkage 0.1 is applied consistently to baseline/combined specifications to control instability at the actual event count. Inspect PH with rank and KM-time transformations, and compare a three-df age spline with the linear baseline on identical validation splits. If >=100 patients and >=70 deaths remain, include a limited random survival forest sensitivity (100 trees, minimum leaf 10, max depth 3, fixed seed); this is an explicitly revised nonlinear comparison. No tuning for a favourable result. Never put final follow-up time into baseline predictors to approximate a time interaction.

## Full-pipeline validation and intervals

Primary point estimate: 10 repeats of patient-grouped five-fold cross-validation, reporting comparable-pair-weighted within-fold Harrell concordance. Every training fold refits the complete development pipeline and each clinical/combined model and Breslow baseline. Fold assignment is outcome-independent from shuffled unique patient IDs; duplicated bootstrap IDs are always kept in one fold. Same folds and validation IDs for all models.

Uncertainty: 500 outer patient bootstrap samples, each evaluated through one complete grouped five-fold validation run, with all development and inner tuning repeated. Report percentile 95% intervals for this cross-validated performance estimator and within-run paired differences. State explicitly that these intervals target the revised cross-validation statistic under bootstrap patient sampling, not an optimism-corrected C-index or an OOB percentile interval. Point repeats and bootstrap use frozen independent seeds 20261014 and 20261015. Preserve every split, tuning record, selected feature, transformation, coefficient, validation prediction, failure and run result. Resume only completed matching-configuration checkpoints. Report attempted/completed/failed runs; no failure-based performance selection. Seed assignment per run deterministic. At least 500 attempted outer replicates; missing comparisons use the same joint-success subset and are disclosed.

Time metrics: 12,24,36 months, training-derived Breslow survival and training-derived censoring distribution via scikit-survival. Test follow-up support and weights before evaluating; unsupported folds are marked unavailable. Average fold metrics with explicit evaluable n. Brier/IBS uses a 1-month grid from months 1 to36 within common support, not four-point quadrature. Calibration uses patient-level averaged cross-validated event probabilities across ten repeats, five prespecified equal-frequency bins, and grouped KM observations; describe this as cross-validated internal calibration. Bootstrap intervals for primary Harrell measures do not imply time-metric intervals. No decision curve is retained without a real action and justified thresholds.

## Integrity tests and release gates

Test no patient overlap; validation outcomes cannot alter training feature selection, tuning, coefficients or transformations; input order and same seed reproducibility; physical volume units; Harrell risk direction; library IPCW definitions. Test fitted score, coefficients and reconstruction against saved patient scores. Save complete source manifest, protocol/config hashes and package/environment lock. Documents preserve author/ethics metadata from actual submission, distinguish previous versus revised estimates and state pending independent review/public release honestly. The final archive must be PENDING_EVIDENCE whenever a material required evidence gate remains unmet.
